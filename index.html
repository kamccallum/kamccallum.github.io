Kyla McCallum

is a student of New Media and Digital Design in NYC. She has two years of experience in graphic design, communications, and community engagement. She is currently the graphic design intern for a nonprofit nature magazine named Orion and the Community Engagement Advisor for an independent boutique in West Michigan. When not tinkering away in Adobe Illustrator, Kyla is probably reading.

Book Cover Redesign

The original cover:

The new cover:

The final assignment of Kyla’s Graphic Design and Digital Tools class was to redesign a book cover of her choice. Kyla decided to redesign The Winter of the Witch by Katherine Arden. She created this cover in Adobe Illustrator with the pen tool. Then, on top of the shapes, she used a Wacom tablet to add texture. The typography of the title and the author’s name was created using her hand lettering skills. After designing the typography on paper, she uploaded an image to Illustrator and used the pen tool to digitize it.

Alexandra Byrne Illustration

This graphic was an assignment for Kyla's Graphic Design and Digital Tools class. Each tile was created in Adobe Illustrator to admire the work of costume designer Alexandra Byrne.

She used the inspiration board above to aid in her design. As you can see, Alexandra Byrne has worked on a number of productions, including many that are period pieces. Kyla chose selectively from Alexandra's work so that the final design would have a clear color palette.

Graphic Design

Holland Museum Social Assets

These graphics were completed for a summer internship in the communications department of the Holland Museum. Interns across all departments of the museum collaborated to plan a small exhibition in the front lobby of the Museum. The square graphic is marketing the opening of the exhibit, titled Greetings from Holland, Michigan: Origins and Impact of the Local Post Office. The graphic below was displayed on the museum website to market the exhibit in general. Both were heavily inspired by vintage postcards. All design and illustrative work was created independently by Kyla.

GMHC Social Assets

These graphic were created for the Gay Men’s Health Center (GMHC). Kyla worked with the director of Women’s Services to develop a tag line and an accompanying visual. The design process started with sketches of the four women on paper, which then led to Adobe Illustrator for digitally rendering the women. The square graphic was intended for Instagram and Facebook, but Kyla also made horizontal versions for LinkedIn and Twitter (displayed below). Please note, the hashtags on the graphic were added at the request of the director.

Orion Event Flyer

Kyla created this flyer for an Orion fundraising event. The marketing team was already using an image of a bluebird, and they wanted to maintain this branding. Kyla decided to illustrate the bluebird and display it prominently on the flyer. She also created variations of this design intended for social media rather than print (see below).

Layout & Data Visualization

The Observer Layouts

The Observer is Fordham Lincoln Center’s online and print newspaper, publishing biweekly. As the Assistant Layout Editor, Kyla was responsible for print layout. She primarily worked on the Fun & Games section of the newspaper, embracing fun graphics and bright color palettes. These are two of her favorite layouts from her time on The Observer editorial board. She illustrated the roses, clock, and rabbit herself.

GMHC Data Flyer

These data visualizations were created for the Gay Men's Health Center to share data about the demographics and needs of their clients. Kyla developed the pie charts in RawGraphs using aggregated data, then improved their design in Adobe Illustrator.
