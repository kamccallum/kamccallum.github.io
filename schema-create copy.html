Content Schema for Locations

Quick Description

  • This content schema can be used to describe any place on Earth.

  • This may prove useful for geographers who are looking up academic resources related to a particular place, but they do not know any details about the place or the preferred name.

  • Below the schema are two examples of how to describe a location according to the outlined requirements.


Learning Outcomes

I very much appreciate the feedback that I received on this assignment. Daniel and Yutaka opened my eyes to some of the ethical issues involved in using a source like Getty; places may have been called something by the original inhabitants that is no longer formally recognized, and some places may be contested, with their independence only partially recognized. Harry also suggested that I include attributes for the climate, so I added average temperature and rainfall to the content schema. I also changed the reference link for Industries to the U.S. Labor Department’s alphabetical list, as their terms seem more comprehensive than ones that I originally provided.
